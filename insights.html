Key Insights

What I learned when the classroom met the real world.

Each insight connects a within-the-classroom (WTC) experience and a beyond-the-classroom (BTC) experience through a specific concept or framework. Each one ends with how it changed my thinking and what it means for my goals, and links to artifacts for both experiences.

01

Research Is About Curiosity and Learning, Not Having a Perfect Answer

I used to picture research as a zombie-movie miracle cure. CSCE 585 and the Supercomputing 2025 Conference showed me that research begins with admitting what I do not know.

WTC · CSCE 585 AlphaEvolve Clone Concept · Epistemic Curiosity BTC · Presenting AutoSlim at SC25
02

Hands-On Experience Turned Knowledge Into Real Skills

I learn by building, testing, and fixing. CSCE 201 made security concepts real, and AutoSlim research with Dr. Rasha Karakchi turned them into skills I can use.

WTC · CSCE 201 Computer Security Framework · Experiential Learning BTC · AutoSlim Research
03

Communication Means Meeting People Where They Are

I started college afraid to speak first. EDLP 520, peer leadership, and my research groups taught me that communication is listening, sharing, and adapting to each person.

WTC · EDLP 520 The Teacher as Manager Concept · Strengths-Based, Adaptive Communication BTC · UNIV 101 Peer Leader
