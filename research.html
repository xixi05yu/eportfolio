Research Posters, Publications & Resume

My research applies machine learning to make computer hardware more efficient, from pruning automata graphs for FPGA accelerators to running neural networks directly on FPGA hardware. Select any poster to view it full size.

Performance and Resource Analysis of MLP Forward Pass on LabVIEW FPGA

  • Jul 23, 2026USC Summer Symposium

A multi-layer perceptron built in fixed-point arithmetic on an NI cRIO-9033 FPGA, using a pipelined matrix-multiplication engine, with an analysis of how latency and DSP usage grow as the network scales.

Download PDF

AutoSlim: Intelligent Automata Graph Optimization for Efficient Acceleration

  • Apr 24, 2026USC Discover Day
  • Nov 18, 2025Supercomputing 2025 (SC25) International Conference

A machine-learning pipeline that prunes redundant states and transitions before FPGA deployment, cutting 30–40% of transitions and reducing LUTs from 47K to 7K on a ZCU104 board.

Download PDF

ML-Based Automata Simplification for Symbolic Accelerators

  • Jul 31, 2025USC Summer Symposium

Using a Random Forest classifier to identify and remove low-impact nodes and edges from automata graphs, shrinking the hardware footprint of symbolic accelerators.

Download PDF

Enhancing Security and Efficiency in Computational Hardware Through Intelligent Optimization (V2)

An updated version of my first poster with expanded results on eliminating weak and potentially malicious nodes before they are mapped onto accelerator hardware.
